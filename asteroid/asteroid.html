How to play

Pick your wager and how many black holes hide in the field, then tap LAUNCH.

Tap asteroids to crack them open. Each one that holds a crystal raises your multiplier (the strip at the top shows every step).

Tap COLLECT whenever you like to bank your wager × the multiplier. Crack a black hole and the wager is gone.

More black holes = a much faster climb, and a much bigger risk. Crack every safe asteroid and you collect automatically.
