Take a Bite: title designs

Three animated title styles, all built in code so they can move. Each takes a bite out of the "E" every few seconds. Tap a title to make it chomp now. The chips below re-colour all three, which is how each new machine would get its own look.

Classic casino neon. The letters flicker now and then like real tubes. Feels retro and night-time.

Thick letters with real depth, a shine that sweeps across, a gentle wave and sparkles. Feels like a big Vegas sign.

take a
tap to chomp

Rounded, glossy letters that jiggle like gummy candy, with little drips. Cute, and the most "alive".

What changes per machine

  • Colours: six colour settings re-skin the whole title (try the chips).
  • Title style: neon, 3D or jelly, each with its own font.
  • Signature move: Take a Bite chomps. Another machine could drip, flicker, spin or melt.
  • Possible extras: reel-frame trim (gold, chrome, candy), the face's eye colour, the bonus name and its music key.
