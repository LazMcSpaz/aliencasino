How to play

Pick a chip, then tap the worlds you want to bet on (tap again to add more). Press SPIN. If the wheel stops on a world you backed, you get your bet × that world's number.

Planetary alignment: before every spin a scanner picks one world and boosts it (×2 to ×10). If that world comes up, its payout is multiplied by the boost.

Wormhole: nobody wins or loses yet. Every bet rides again on a free spin, paying double. Two wormholes in a row: ×4.

Black hole: swallows every bet on the table.

Over a long time the wheel pays back about 96% of what goes in.
