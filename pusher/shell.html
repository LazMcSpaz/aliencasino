How to play

Tap above the pusher to drop a coin there (hold to keep dropping). Pick the coin's value below the machine first.

Coins land on the sliding pusher. When it pulls back they're scraped off onto the tray, and when it comes forward it shoves the whole pile.

Everything that falls over the front edge is yours. The two corner gutters keep what falls in them. Coins pile up on top of each other and tip over when the pile below moves.

Prizes turn up on the pusher now and then. They pay a multiple of the coin value you were playing when they appeared:

Your pile is kept between visits. Over a long time the machine pays back about 95% of what goes in.
