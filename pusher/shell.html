How to play

Tap over the machine to drop a gold bar (1 bar each) onto the sliding pusher, left or right where you tap (hold to keep dropping).

When the pusher pulls back, the wall behind it scrapes the bars off onto the tray. When it comes forward, it shoves the whole pile. Bars stack, slide and tip over like real ones.

Everything that falls over the front edge is yours. The two corner gutters keep what falls in them.

Crystals turn up now and then instead of a bar. Push one over the edge to win it:

Slapping: Zib keeps watch up top. When the guard looks away the screen goes green: CLEAR! You get two slaps on the side of the machine to shake bars loose. Slap while the guard is watching and security fines you 15 bars.

Your pile is kept between visits. Over a long time the machine pays back about 95% of what goes in.
