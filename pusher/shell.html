How to play

Tap over the machine to drop a gold bar onto the sliding pusher, left or right where you tap (hold to keep dropping). Pick the bar's value first.

When the pusher pulls back, the wall behind it scrapes the bars off onto the tray. When it comes forward, it shoves the whole pile. Bars stack, slide and tip over like real ones.

Everything that falls over the front edge is yours. The two corner gutters keep what falls in them.

Crystals turn up now and then instead of a bar. They pay a multiple of the bar value you were playing when they appeared:

Your pile is kept between visits. Over a long time the machine pays back about 95% of what goes in.
