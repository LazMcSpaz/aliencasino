How to play

Tap a section of the bowl to put a chip on it. Then SPIN: the comet circles the rim and spirals down into the black hole.

Each time it drops over the bottom edge of a tier, the section it crosses is that tier's result, and bets on it pay straight away. The thin dark slivers are voids: if the comet crosses one, that tier's bets lose.

Path: tap PATH, then pick one section on each tier. If the comet goes through all three, it pays ×1250.

Void bet: tap the black hole to bet that the comet crosses a void.
