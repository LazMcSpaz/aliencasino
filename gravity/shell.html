How to play

Tap a section of the bowl to put a chip on it. Then SPIN: the comet circles the rim and spirals down into the black hole.

Each time it drops over the bottom edge of a tier, the section it crosses is that tier's result, and bets on it pay straight away. The thin dark slivers are voids: if the comet crosses one, that tier's bets lose.

Path bonus: if the comet passes through one of your bets on every tier, your smallest winning chip is multiplied by the path bonus, on top of your normal wins. The fewer sections you cover, the bigger the bonus (shown next to the chips).

Frenzy: every so often (about one spin in 18, completely at random) the launcher fires three comets instead of one. Every comet pays on your bets, for the price of one spin.

Colour bets: tap the coloured band around the outside. You win if the comet stays in that colour on all three tiers.

Void bet: tap the black hole to bet that the comet crosses a void.
