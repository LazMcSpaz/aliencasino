How to play

Pay your bet to start a climb. Each DROP spins the next row sideways like a reel. Stones that stop over thin air crumble.

Skim: between drops, tap a stone worth 2 or more to pocket half its value right away. It cracks, and every crack adds to the chance the tower falls on each drop — and a skim can shatter the tower on the spot.

Reach a band to cash out (band × everything stacked) or keep climbing. Keep climbing and collapse, and you get nothing.
