How to play

Set your bet with the chips, then tap BET before the launch.

The ship takes off and the multiplier climbs: ×1.5, ×2, ×10… Tap CASH OUT any time to win your bet × the multiplier.

But the warp core can blow at any moment, even right on the launch pad. If it blows before you cash out, you lose your bet.

AUTO cashes you out by itself when the ship reaches the multiplier you set. Tap the middle to turn it on or off.

The crew are other players. Watch them bail out, or get greedy and go down with the ship.
