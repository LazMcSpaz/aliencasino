How to play

Set your wager with the chips, then tap BET before the drone launches.

The drone lifts off and the multiplier climbs: ×1.5, ×2, ×10… Tap BAIL any time to collect your wager × the multiplier.

But the drone can explode at any moment, even on the launch pad. If it explodes before you bail, you lose your wager.

AUTO BAIL collects for you when the drone reaches the multiplier you set. Tap the middle to turn it on or off.

The flight board shows the other players. Watch who bails, and who rides it too long.
