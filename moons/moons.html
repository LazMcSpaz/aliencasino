How to play

Roll two 8-sided moon crystals: tap ROLL, or press and hold it to pick them up, slide up and flick to throw.

What counts is the gap between them. If they match, they collide: that's the crash.

First roll: a 1 and an 8 (gap 7, "full orbit") wins straight away. A collision loses. Any other gap becomes the point.

Then: keep rolling. Roll the point's gap again and WITH wins. Collide first and AGAINST wins.

Gap bets (the row of numbers) win if that gap comes up before a collision. COLLIDE bets the very next roll is a match.

Orbit streak (place it before a run starts) pays if you hit 3 or more points in a row before a collision: the more points, the bigger the payout.
